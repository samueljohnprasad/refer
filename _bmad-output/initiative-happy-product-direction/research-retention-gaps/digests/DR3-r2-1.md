## Copy rules derivable (inference from 1-5, not a quoted source)
- No method/location detail, ever (1,2,5).
- Say "died by suicide", never "committed"/"successful" (1,3).
- Every crisis surface includes a reachable helpline (5,6; region-specific: US 988 + text HOME 741741; UK Samaritans 116 123 + text SHOUT 85258 + 999/111; IN Tele-MANAS 14416 / 1-800-891-4416).
- Hopeful, recovery-oriented framing; don't imply suicide is common or inevitable (1,4,5).
- Don't attribute to a single cause (1).
- Do NOT list KIRAN as a separate line (14).
