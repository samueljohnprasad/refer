## Patterns
- Timing anchored to the user's own habitual time (Duolingo: 30 min before; 23.5h after last session), not fixed clock.
- Hard stop after non-response: Duolingo sends ~8, then an explicit "we'll stop" message, then only seasonal campaigns. Self-limiting is evidenced; tone of stop message drew "guilt" backlash per snippets (debugger.medium.com).
- Return moment = celebration + choice (refresher vs. new path), not a recap of what was missed.
- Mindfulness apps: one daily, user-scheduled, opt-in reminder; content-led copy (micro-insight / question) rather than "you missed X". Headspace prefix label "Mindful Moment:".
- Companion apps (Finch): absence carries no penalty; character "waits". Health apps (Oura/Gentler Streak): explicit rest states legitimize pausing.
- Guilt/fear framing ("you're scaring me") exists in Duolingo but is the cited source of user complaints — risky for mental-health audience (inference).
