## Leads

- Price band for consumer self-guided: ~$4.99–$19.99/mo, $17.99–$99.99/yr; anchor cluster $69.99–$79.99/yr (Headspace, Calm, Youper, Wysa low SKU).
- Trackers (Daylio, Bearable) price far lower (~$18–$50/yr); How We Feel fully free — free tier must beat a $0 Yale-backed competitor on emotion logging.
- Two CBT-chatbot exits (Woebot consumer, Sanvello consumer) → consumer self-guided CBT space thinner; FDA route cited as cost driver for Woebot.
- Multiple same-name SKUs (Finch 9, Bearable 10, Calm 6) = active price testing / regional or intro offers.
