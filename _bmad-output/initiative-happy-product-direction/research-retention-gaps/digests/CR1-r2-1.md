## Patterns (from evidence above only)
- Mental-wellness pushes seen are content-first, not guilt: Headspace's are a branded series ("Mindful Moment:" prefix) of self-compassion lines with no CTA; Flo's is a soft question ("A little time for yourself today?").
- Finch splits reminders into named, separately toggleable categories, with streak reminders as just one opt-out-able category alongside "Good vibes" and character messages ("Messages from Lee"); per-task reminders support Snooze/Skip and "I already did this".
- Permission pre-prompts frame value as scheduling or accountability ("When would you like to practice?", "Stay accountable"), not as notifications.
- Streak-loss copy in non-wellness apps is blunt ("1 Day streak lost", "Skip a day, and it resets"); Insight Timer at zero is neutral/forward ("Practice now to make progress...").
- No after-absence (day 7/14/30) copy surfaced for any wellness app this run.
