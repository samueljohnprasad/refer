## Synthesis (inference, not evidence)
- Guideline dose anchor: ~6–8 structured sessions over ~9–12 weeks, then a relapse-prevention/staying-well plan (#1–3, two-sourced). A defensible trigger for offering maintenance mode is "core course done (~6–8 sessions-equivalent over ≥8–12 weeks) AND symptoms improved", with the staying-well plan as the hand-off.
- Healthy disengagement = e-attainment (#7) + "effective engagement" (#6): stop because goals met, not because the app failed. Distinguish via a goal/symptom check at lapse, not usage counts (#7, #9).
- Maintenance should be optional, user-timed, and self-directed (#13); expect ~1/3 uptake. Relapse risk is high (#4, #14), so maintenance needs a re-entry path back to daily practice.
