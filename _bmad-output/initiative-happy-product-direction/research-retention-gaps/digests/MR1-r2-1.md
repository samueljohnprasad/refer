## Synthesis (evidence-bounded)
- Store-wide MH-app D30 converges on ~3% median from two independent primary panels (Baumel 2019 panel; Kaveladze 2022 Apptopia). Category-level H&F D30 from vendors (3–8%) still only via aggregators; vendor originals unread.
- Organic vs paid gap (~2x at D30) appears in aggregator echo of AppsFlyer only — treat as unverified.
- Trial-recruited CBT chatbot users retain far higher (70% D30) — a ceiling, not a benchmark.
