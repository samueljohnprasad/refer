### Implications (I)
- PHQ-4 is validated, free, scored 0-12 with 2x subscales; fit for a brief periodic check. Its 2-week recall window means administering more often than ~every 2 weeks changes the construct; for frequent checks, evidence supports daily/EMA-adapted PHQ-2/PHQ-9 (Bauer 2018, Haddox 2026) but app scores may run higher than clinic scores (Torous 2015) — do not compare in-app scores to clinical cut-offs without caution.
- PHQ-4 has no suicide item; app needs a separate risk pathway (e.g. PHQ-9 item 9 or dedicated question with crisis-resource screen) if it wants to detect risk.
- 0-100% belief rating has practitioner and process-research use, but this run found no psychometric validation as an outcome measure: treat as engagement/process signal, not outcome.

## Leads
- Read Löwe 2010 full text for exact alpha and PHQ-4 normative percentiles; read the UKE "Update of the standardization of the PHQ-4" (Kliem/Hinz et al.?) for current norms.
- Fetch https://www.phqscreeners.com terms page directly to confirm licensing wording.
- Torous 2015 full text: how item 9 disclosures were handled.
- Search for studies of belief-rating change predicting symptom change (e.g. within-session belief change, Kazantzis homework literature; Thought Challenger / IntelliCare).
- Furukawa group HCT / FLATT papers: whether belief % data were analysed.
- PHQ-2 vs PHQ-9 in measurement-based care: Kroenke 2003 PHQ-2 paper; Löwe 2004 PHQ-9 change scores (minimal clinically important difference).
