# Deepening Brief: Day-0 Activation, Aha Moment, and Premium Intent

**Approved:** 2026-10-30  
**Intent:** Deepen the existing competitive research  
**Decision:** Choose the Day-0 sequence that gives a first-time mental-health user a genuine CBT aha moment, preserves trust, and creates earned premium intent.

## Candidates

1. Current-style canned Thought Catcher followed by reward, reminder request, and paywall.
2. Value-first sequence with a short lesson and a personally relevant Thought Catcher before Home.
3. Another evidence-supported first-win sequence identified during research.

## Research questions

- What do current leading onboarding flows actually show, and which screens deliver product value rather than personalization theatre?
- What credible evidence exists about paywall timing, trial conversion, renewal, refunds, and retention?
- Does self-relevant cognitive reappraisal create more learning or perceived value than observing a canned example?
- When do rewards, commitment devices, reminders, and monetization support autonomy, and when do they create reactance?
- What do users praise or reject in mental-health onboarding and subscription flows?
- Which measurable event should define Happy's first useful loop and premium trigger?

## Evidence plan

- **Competitive flow track:** Current Mobbin iOS flows for Duolingo, Finch, Headspace, Calm, Ahead, stoic, Liven, Fabulous, and relevant CBT products. Screens prove only observed design behavior, not business outcomes.
- **Monetization and user-voice track:** Primary subscription benchmarks, official platform guidance, current pricing/paywall documentation, direct review evidence, and recent public user discussions.
- **Academic track:** Primary papers and systematic reviews on cognitive reappraisal, self-reference, active learning, autonomy/reactance, implementation intentions, rewards, and digital mental-health engagement.

## Verification rules

- Every factual claim needs publisher, publication date, access date, and direct URL.
- Load-bearing performance or capability claims require primary evidence or two independent sources.
- Project documents and previous conclusions shape questions only; they are not external evidence.
- Unsupported legacy percentages are downgraded or overturned.
- Thin or absent public evidence is reported as such.
