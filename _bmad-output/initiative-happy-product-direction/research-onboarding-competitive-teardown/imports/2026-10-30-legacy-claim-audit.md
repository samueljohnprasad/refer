**Purpose:** Identify claims requiring fresh verification before they influence the onboarding decision.

## Structural finding

The legacy appendix has three rows, but each row bundles several publishers and multiple unrelated claims behind a single citation marker. It provides no direct article, report, flow, review, or dataset URLs. Consequently, the appendix cannot establish that a cited publisher made any specific numerical or causal claim.

## Claims that Mobbin can establish

Mobbin captures can establish observed sequence, copy, controls, optionality, and screen placement in a captured app version. They cannot establish conversion, retention, psychological mechanism, clinical benefit, or user sentiment.

## Provisional status

All numerical and causal legacy claims above are **unverified** until supported by the 2026-10-30 evidence tracks. Any that cannot be traced will be explicitly overturned as usable evidence, even if their directional intuition remains plausible.
