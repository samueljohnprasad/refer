- (direction) Use express distillation from the approved Happy product brief; preserve the brief as the narrative source and derive the spec from this log.
- (decision) Happy's product contract is a guided mental-health learning and practice loop: Learn -> Practise -> Journal -> Notice patterns -> Continue.
- (capability) CAP-1 intent: Users can complete short educational lessons about anxiety, stress, sleep, mindfulness, and related topics to understand one concept and identify a next practice; success: a demonstration shows a user can start and finish a lesson and reach its linked next step.
- (capability) CAP-2 intent: Users can choose and complete a guided CBT or coping practice relevant to the lesson or current need; success: a practice can be started, completed, and visibly marked as complete.
- (capability) CAP-3 intent: Users can create a guided reflection through supported text, voice, photo, or prompt-based input; success: a user can complete a reflection and retrieve it later in their private journal.
- (capability) CAP-4 intent: Users can review time-based reflections and recognise recurring emotions, triggers, habits, or coping patterns; success: after sufficient entries, the product demonstrates a dated insight or pattern with a clear time window and no diagnostic conclusion.
- (capability) CAP-5 intent: Users can resume their learning and practice journey with progress preserved and one clear next action; success: after completing any stage, a user can return and continue without losing progress.
- (capability) CAP-6 intent: Users can understand what Happy provides and does not provide before relying on it; success: user-facing boundary and privacy messaging is present where needed and excludes diagnosis, treatment replacement, crisis care, and guaranteed outcomes.
- (constraint) Happy targets iOS 26+ and the first contract must not require Android or older-iOS behavior.
- (constraint) Happy is self-guided education and support, not diagnosis, professional treatment, therapy replacement, or crisis intervention.
- (constraint) Only capabilities available and verified in the released build may be marketed; unsupported clinical efficacy, expert endorsement, privacy, encryption, and outcome claims are prohibited.
- (constraint) Reflections and health-adjacent information are sensitive; the product must not encourage public disclosure or expose private content through social mechanisms.
- (constraint) Gamification may support consistency but must not trivialise distress, turn mental health into competition, or become proof of benefit.
- (constraint) The freemium model must not block the user's first meaningful learning-practice-reflection loop solely to force a subscription decision.
- (constraint) Non-goal: Happy will not present assessment as diagnosis or claim to cure anxiety, guarantee relief, or replace therapy.
- (constraint) Non-goal: Happy will not provide crisis intervention, emergency care, or personalised medication or treatment advice.
- (constraint) Non-goal: Happy will not become an open-ended AI therapist or a social feed for sensitive disclosures.
- (constraint) Non-goal: This contract does not establish clinical efficacy, medical-system integration, or health-record workflows.
- (constraint) Non-goal: Acquisition, streaks, and subscriptions will not be treated as evidence of clinical benefit.
- (assumption) The spec serves product direction and downstream planning, not an investor pitch or clinical efficacy submission.
- (assumption) Voice and photo reflection are supported only where the released build and current privacy disclosures verify them; otherwise they are gated or described as future capability.
- (assumption) Current activation, retention, conversion, and subscription baselines are unknown and must be measured before numerical targets are set.
- (assumption) The first user journey should make anxiety, overthinking, sleep, and general self-understanding available, but the strongest acquisition and activation wedge is not yet chosen.

- (question) How should high-risk medical or self-harm content be detected, escalated, and kept outside automated product guidance?
- (question) What subscription boundaries preserve the first meaningful loop while supporting the freemium business model?

- (decision) The first-time experience is one focused activation loop: silent anonymous Supabase session, plain-language goal choice, one matching lesson, one related CBT practice, one text reflection, immediate feedback, then Home; it does not expose every existing screen before value is demonstrated.
- (constraint) There is no separate guest mode. Anonymous Supabase auth starts at first launch, and later signup or login links the provider identity to the same user so progress, practices, journal records, and insights remain continuous.
- (decision) Existing Journeys, courses, CBT tools, coping cards, journal capture, journal history, and insight surfaces are preserved. StarterPath is a first-use routing layer over the existing Journey system, not a replacement for the course catalog or direct tool access.
- (decision) After the first loop, Home exposes the recurring loop: resume the current path, repeat a learned tool, start the next lesson, reflect in the journal, or review an available insight. Tools remain reusable after first exposure; the product does not give one tool and stop.
- (decision) Recurring product evidence records practice start/completion, reflection acknowledgement, structured observation projection, readiness state, and next action. Analytics records metadata only and never raw reflection text, audio, photos, or health-adjacent free text.
- (decision) Activity summary readiness requires 3 distinct completed practice sessions in a rolling 7-day window. Pattern readiness requires at least 5 relevant structured observations across at least 3 distinct user-local days in a rolling 30-day window. Until ready, the insight surface shows insufficient data.

- (decision) The paywall must not interrupt the first lesson-practice-reflection loop. Premium gates may appear when the user deliberately selects premium courses, tools, limits, or advanced surfaces after activation.
