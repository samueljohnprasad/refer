## Second path: feel less overwhelmed

`feel_less_overwhelmed`

| Stage | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | Feel less overwhelmed |
| Lesson | Stress is a signal |
| Learning outcome | The user can name one current stress signal and choose one small next action without treating stress as a diagnosis. |
| Practice | Grounding Reset, a short sensory grounding exercise. Confirm its released-build exercise identifier before implementation. |
| Reflection | Private text check-in: “What did you notice, and what is one small thing you want to do next?” |
| Home next action | After lesson completion, resume Grounding Reset. After practice completion, resume the private reflection. After reflection completion, return Home to the recurring loop while preserving progress for both paths. |
| Activation success | The user selects this goal, completes the lesson and practice, saves one private text reflection, and returns to Home with the next action preserved. |

## Boundaries

- Each path owns its goal, canonical lesson, linked practice, reflection prompt, and course enrollment target; do not replace these with a hardcoded default.
- The lesson target and progress must resolve through the canonical Journeys course system rather than a parallel starter-only lesson state.
- Use educational and supportive language only.
- Do not promise calm, relief, control, or symptom reduction.
- Do not diagnose, assess crisis risk, prescribe treatment, or replace professional care.
- Keep reflection content private and out of analytics; record metadata only.
- The path is reversible and must not restrict access to other courses or tools.
