- (decision) Position Happy as guided mental-health learning plus practice: short courses, CBT tools, journaling, and pattern insights.
- (decision) Primary audience is adults and young adults who want to understand anxious or stressful thoughts and build a repeatable daily practice.
- (decision) Core loop is Learn -> Practise -> Journal -> Notice patterns -> Continue.
- (decision) Brand promise is Understand your mind. Practice what helps. Happy is supportive self-help, not diagnosis, therapy replacement, or a guaranteed treatment.
- (assumption) The current brief is for product direction and downstream planning, not an investor pitch or a clinical efficacy submission.
