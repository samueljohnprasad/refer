## Q7a — Early paywall / trial / pricing complaints

### Theme A1: "Free trial" that charged immediately or without warning (auto-charge anger)
- "start your 7 days free trial" and instead of a free period I was instantly charged 79,99€" — Finch, Trustpilot, 2023-04-24 (older), https://www.trustpilot.com/review/finchcare.com
- "being told they would sent a notification before the end of the trial. Never got one and got charged 80 euros" — Finch, Trustpilot, 2025-10-01, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/finchcare.com
- "Signed up for a free trial and the immediately took money from my account!" — Headspace, Trustpilot UK, 2024-12-20 (older), https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/headspace.com?page=6
- "Signed up for the "free 14 days"... tried to take £49.99 from my account." — Headspace, Trustpilot UK, 2025-02-07 (older), same URL
- "didn't realize my free trial was over" — Reflectly, App Store (aggregator, undated), https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1241229134/reflectly/reviews
[distinct voices: 5, across 3 apps, 2 platforms]
Published quant: Appbot analysis of 40,294 Finch reviews (Aug 2025–Aug 2026): ~6.7% of negative/mixed sample cite free-trial auto-charges; pricing complaints 27% iOS / 21% Play of negative/mixed. https://appbot.co/blog/finch-app-reviews-emotional-attachment-user-retention-product-loyalty/
Confidence: HIGH — multiple apps, Trustpilot + app-store aggregator + quantified review-mining.

### Theme A2: Paywall offered before user knows what the app/plan is
- "offered a 'free trial' OR discounted subscription for Finch Plus (without knowing what Finch plus means & never having used the app)" — Finch, Trustpilot, 2026-03-06, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/finchcare.com
- "I didn't want to pay for additional features but somehow was upgraded to Plus. This was not clear to me" — Finch, Trustpilot, 2026-02-13, same URL
- "Then my hope was crushed when I was met with a paywall… Was heavily under impression it was just a simple journal app." — Earkick, App Store (aggregator, undated), https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1584854531/earkick-mental-health-tracker/reviews
- "locked after one AI chat. ...please just tell us that upfront" — Earkick, same URL
[distinct voices: 4, 2 apps]
Context (not user voice): Calm teardown reports two paywalls back-to-back before any content, third after sign-in — https://screensdesign.com/articles/calm-onboarding-design/ (vendor teardown, inference about UX, not reviews).
Confidence: MEDIUM — clear in two apps; prevalence across category unproven.

### Theme A3: "Paywalling help" moral framing — help apps should serve those who can't afford help
- "I just thought that the point of all these help apps coming out were to help the people that can't afford to get professional help." — Earkick, App Store (aggregator, undated), https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1584854531/earkick-mental-health-tracker/reviews
- "don't have the money to pay yearly for an app that I used to love" — Reflectly, App Store (aggregator, undated), https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1241229134/reflectly/reviews
- Low-weight (unattributed): "The app is supposed to help with anxiety, but the subscription price gives me anxiety." — unstar.app 2026 blog, https://unstar.app/blog/mental-health-app-reviews-what-users-say-about-wellbeing-apps-2026
Study: JMIR preprint 97517 (depression/anxiety app reviews) lists "pricing structure, subscription gatekeeping" among top concerns — via search snippet only; full text not retrievable this run. https://preprints.jmir.org/preprint/97517/accepted
Confidence: MEDIUM — 2 attributed voices + study snippet; study not read in full.

### Theme A4: Free tier gutted / constant upsell
- "intense lack of content for non-premium users" — Reflectly, aggregator, undated, https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1241229134/reflectly/reviews
- "constantly bugged with 'so you want to upgrade?' notifications" — Reflectly, same
- Wysa: users "expressed frustration with the dwindling free content" — ChoosingTherapy review (updated 2026-10-13, paraphrase of users), https://www.choosingtherapy.com/wysa-app-review/
[distinct voices: 2 direct + 1 reviewer paraphrase]
Confidence: MEDIUM.

### Theme A5: What users consider fair — usable free core, optional paid extras, pay-what-you-can
- Wysa praised as it "isn't blocked by a paywall unlike some other apps" — user quote via search snippet (aggregator), https://makerstack.co/reviews/wysa-review/ (not directly fetched)
- Finch reviewers value "the paid version is optional" and guardian/gift memberships for those who cannot afford — Appbot summary, https://appbot.co/blog/finch-app-reviews-emotional-attachment-user-retention-product-loyalty/
- "Earkick IS FREE!! Granted, Earkick has a few different features that do require payment but I think it is worth trying" — Earkick, aggregator, https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1584854531/earkick-mental-health-tracker/reviews
- Finch "Can't Afford It" free access reported by multiple Reddit users — second-hand via search snippet (whistleout/androidauthority article), not verified on Reddit.
Confidence: MEDIUM — consistent across 3 apps but mostly secondhand.

### Theme A6: Lifetime vs subscription
- Daylio: App Store reviewers appreciate a one-off payment / "not requiring a subscription" — search-engine summary only; no verbatim quote retrieved. Daylio offers lifetime $59.99 (https://intuitionlabs.ai/software/telepsychiatry-digital-mental-health/mood-tracking-and-journaling/daylio).
- Retention-offer anger (unattributed, low weight): "After cancelling, they offered me 60% off... I feel scammed." — unstar.app 2026.

## Q7b — Onboarding complaints and good first sessions

### Theme B1: Forced login / account lost data
- "had to login, which I've never had to do before" / "when I logged back on everything was gone" — Reflectly, aggregator, undated, https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1241229134/reflectly/reviews
[distinct voices: 1–2, one app]
Confidence: LOW.

### Theme B2: Praise for no-account / anonymous start
- Wysa: "You will not have to create an account to use Wysa, all you have to enter is a nickname." (vendor statement) and "You remain anonymous" listed as a pro — ChoosingTherapy, 2026-10-13, https://www.choosingtherapy.com/wysa-app-review/
- Rootd founder: "There's no hard paywall. You can go and you can get value from it immediately." — vendor, via search snippet (Subscription League podcast/AppFollow), not user voice.
Confidence: LOW as user voice (reviewer/vendor, not users).

### Theme B3: Long quizzes before paywall
No user complaints retrieved. Only industry/teardown material: quiz funnels of 29–52 screens used to build sunk cost before paywall (https://web2appworld.pages.dev/breakdowns/luvly), Headspace's 3-question ~1 min onboarding cited as best practice (https://goodux.appcues.com/blog/headspaces-mindful-onboarding-sequence). INFERENCE: users likely tolerate short personalization; evidence of user anger at long quizzes not found.
Confidence: LOW.
