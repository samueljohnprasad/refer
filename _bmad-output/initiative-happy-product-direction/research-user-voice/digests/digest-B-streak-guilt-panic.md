## Key Design Signals for Happy

1. **Streak guilt peaks at re-entry** — not when streak breaks but when user SEES the broken state. Happy must intercept at that exact moment.
2. **All-or-nothing is the core enemy** — any binary success/failure mechanic will trigger deletion for ADHD/anxiety users.
3. **Zero cognitive load is the bar during acute panic** — 1 tap max. No menus, no reading.
4. **Sensory/somatic > cognitive during panic** — cold water, sour taste, movement, social presence all beat CBT reframing.
5. **"Obligation to perform" framing kills retention** — journaling must feel like it serves the user, not vice versa.
6. **Streak repair must feel earned, not cheating** — compassion framing, not bypass framing.
