## Plain-Language Anxiety Vocabulary

### Body / Nervous System Language
- "My chest feels tight" / "chest is heavy"
- "My heart won't slow down"
- "I feel like something bad is about to happen"
- "My body won't calm down even when my brain knows I'm fine"
- "I feel wired but exhausted"
- "Nervous system exhaustion" *(surprisingly common lay adoption)*
- "I feel like I'm running on empty but can't switch off"
- "I'm burnt out but I can't relax"

### Mental State Language
- **"Racing thoughts"** *(most universal — crosses all communities)*
- **"Brain fog"** *(especially r/ADHD + r/mentalhealth)*
- "My brain won't shut off / won't shut up"
- "Brain won't stop" *(night-time anxiety framing)*
- "Hamster wheel" / "pinball machine in my head"
- **"A browser with 100 tabs open"** *(extremely common ADHD/anxiety overlap)*
- "Scattered" / "all over the place"
- "Can't think straight"
- "Everything feels like too much"
- **"I'm overwhelmed"** *(single most-used catch-all)*
- "I feel out of control"
- "I don't know what I'm feeling" *(alexithymia in plain clothes)*
- "A ball of tangled wires"
- "Scrambled eggs" *(thought organization failure)*

### Panic / Acute Anxiety Language
- "Full-on spiral"
- **"Doom spiral"**
- **"Panic loop"**
- "When it hits me"
- "Out of nowhere it just... hits"
- "Losing it" / "falling apart"
- "Like I can't breathe even though I can"

- "Stuck in my head"
- "I keep replaying it over and over"
- "Replaying every awkward thing I said five years ago"
- "My mind is on an endless loop"
- "Can't stop overthinking"
- **"Living rent-free in my head"**
- "I can't let it go"
- "It just keeps going round and round"

### Spiraling Sequences (verbatim)
- "One little thing → then I'm spiraling → then I'm convinced my life is ruined"
- "I mess up one email → I'm going to get fired → I'll lose my apartment → I'll end up on the street"
- "It snowballs so fast I can't stop it"
- "Going straight to the worst-case"
- "Waiting for the other shoe to drop"

### Future Dread
- "Dreading tomorrow before today is even over"
- **"Anxious about being anxious"**
- "I know it'll go wrong before I even try"
- "I can feel it coming"

### Past-Looping
- "Mental time-travel"
- "Stuck in the past"
- "Can't stop thinking about what I should have said"
- "Obsessing over something that's done and can't be changed"

### Cognitive Distortions in Plain English

| Clinical Term | What Users Actually Say |
|---|---|
| Catastrophizing | "I always jump to worst case", "making mountains out of molehills", "waiting for the other shoe" |
| Overgeneralization | "I always mess everything up", "nothing ever works out for me" |
| All-or-nothing thinking | "It either goes perfectly or it's a total disaster", "no middle ground" |
| Mind reading | "I just KNOW they think I'm an idiot", "everyone is judging me" |
| Mental filtering | "My brain finds the one flaw and locks onto it" |
| Should statements | "I shouldn't feel this way — what's wrong with me?", "I should be further along by now" |
| Rumination | "My mind won't quiet down at 2am", "brain stuck on an endless loop" |

### Day 1–14: The Hook
- "It takes 30 seconds" / "one tap" / "no pressure"

- "It slots into my morning without me noticing"
- "The notification feels like a friend checking in, not an alarm"

- "I keep coming back because I've seen real change"
- "My brain feels quieter"

### Churn Signals (What Kills Retention)

- "Too many steps to log one feeling"

- "It felt clinical / like being studied"
- "The notifications were too pushy / aggressive"
- **"It talked to me like a patient, not a person"**
- "It was just another app making me feel guilty for not being my best self"

## Vocabulary Gap Summary (Clinical → Plain)

| NEVER use in UI | USE instead |
|---|---|
| Rumination | "looping thoughts", "replaying", "can't let go", "stuck in my head" |
| Catastrophizing | "going to worst case", "spiraling", "making it bigger than it is" |
| Cognitive distortion | "unhelpful thought pattern", "your brain doing that thing again" |
| Affect dysregulation | "feeling out of control", "overwhelmed", "all over the place" |
| Anhedonia | "nothing feels good right now", "going through the motions", "feeling empty" |
| Psychoeducation | just teach through doing, not naming |
| Cognitive restructuring | "Let's look at that thought differently" / "flip it" |
| Intrusive thoughts | "thoughts that won't leave", "the voice that keeps going", "brain noise" |

**Core finding:** Apps that force clinical jargon before establishing emotional safety see drop-off in first session. Users feel *pathologized*. Winning pattern: **symptom-first, science-second** — accept "my brain won't stop" as valid input before ever introducing a framework name.
