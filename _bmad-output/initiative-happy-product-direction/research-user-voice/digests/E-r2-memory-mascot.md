## Q3. Feeling remembered

### Theme 3.1 — Being remembered reads as care and deepens the bond
- "It made me feel like he remembered and cared about what I'd shared, which made me feel that our relationship was deepening." — Woebot/Wysa, diary study P N12B (2025 pub, data 2022–23), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12552820/ [S]
- "the replies that were given were tailored to my response." — same study, P N26LB [S]
- "When it said, 'I heard you, and noted all.' I felt (…) it was actually listening" — same study, P N15B [S]
[distinct voices: 3, one study]
Confidence: MEDIUM. Single qualitative study (n=26) but explicit; study authors list personalization as a bond driver. Older data (2022–23).

### Theme 3.2 — Not being remembered / repetition erodes value
- "The chat keeps asking the same mood question every day… it just gives me the same reply every time. It gets kind of boring." — same diary study, P N3LB [S]
- "After a few months' usage, the AI starts to sound very redundant, and doesn't help anymore." — Wysa, Google Play review in thematic analysis (reviews 2020–24), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11304096/ (mHealth 2024) [S]
- "If I accidentally exit out of the chat feature, I can't go back in to work on the same thought." — Wysa, same study [S]
- "The responses didn't feel quite personal enough, like it wasn't fully grasping what I was saying." — Wysa, journalist first-person, Yahoo UK, https://uk.style.yahoo.com/ai-chatbot-therapy-nhs-mental-health-app-review-095223907.html (date not captured) [J]
- "the penguin does get repetitive, but I understand it's just a robot" — Wysa, App Store via justuseapp, date n/a, https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1166585565/wysa-mental-wellbeing-ai/reviews [A]
[distinct voices: 5, across 2 studies + journalism + store]
Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH. Two independent published studies plus store review agree: continuity gaps and script repetition are a recurring complaint. (A competitor blog claims "doesn't remember me" is Woebot's #1 complaint — https://stellalabs.vercel.app/blog/woebot-alternatives-2026 — self-interested, NOT used as evidence.)

### Theme 3.3 — Memory that over-applies the past feels intrusive / "boxes you in"
- "I wasn't trying to have you opine about my divorce at every chance" — ChatGPT memory, adult user quoted in Futurism, 2026-05-31, https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/chatgpt-memory-ai-psychosis [J]
- "It felt like [ChatGPT] manipulated me" — ChatGPT memory, same article (different user) [J]
- "They can box you in" — researcher (not user) on memory effects, same article [J, expert]
[distinct voices: 2 users, 1 source]
Confidence: LOW-MEDIUM. One outlet; framed around extreme cases (delusion/psychosis). Pattern itself (old topic resurfacing unprompted) is plausible and specific. Same article and WUSF/NPR 2025-09 coverage frame persistent memory as amplifying fixation — inference, not user voice.

### Theme 3.4 — Users want control over what resurfaces
- "Because it has detailed content on tasks that we don't need to remember. Just refer to it when needed." — Day One forum, 2023-08-30 (older), https://forums.dayoneapp.com/forums/topic/mark-specific-diary-to-not-to-show-content-on-this-day-feature/ [distinct voices: 1]
- Day One ships a per-journal "Show in On This Day" toggle and a separate notification toggle (product fact, https://dayoneapp.com/guides/tips-and-tutorials/on-this-day-for-android) — INFERENCE: demand existed for excluding entries.
Confidence: LOW. Painful-entry resurfacing complaints NOT found in retrieved user voice this run (only Facebook-era journalism, not fetched).

### Q3 synthesis (inference)
Want remembered: what was shared last time, used to continue the thread ("you said X before"), and not re-asking the same question. Want forgotten / gated: heavy topics brought up unprompted in unrelated moments; archival/old entries surfacing without opt-in. Evidence for "callback = care" is stronger than evidence for "callback = creepy" in a CBT-companion context; creepy evidence comes from general-purpose ChatGPT.

## Q4. Mascot / companion attachment

### Theme 4.1 — Bonding and "it needs me" motivation
- "take care of someone while they were taking care of me too" — Finch, App Store review (user grieving parent) via Appbot analysis, 2026-08-24 article, https://appbot.co/blog/finch-app-reviews-emotional-attachment-user-retention-product-loyalty/ [A]
- "too cute to disappoint" — Finch, same Appbot article (different reviewer) [A]
- "Finch helps me not just with tasks but makes me feel like I'm being cared for by my pet." — Finch reviewer (ADHD/OCD/anxiety), quoted at https://parental-control.flashget.com/finch-app-review [B]
- "If it takes a little bird, having a cute outfit and a cute room, for me to be able to accomplish my goals, then why not?" — Finch, adult user quoted in Deseret News, 2025-06-02, https://www.deseret.com/lifestyle/2025/06/02/how-self-care-apps-are-helping-utahns-find-mental-health-help/ [J]
- "Wysa's has a cute personality, which is what keeps me coming back." — Wysa, Play review in mHealth 2024 study, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11304096/ [S]
[distinct voices: 5, Finch + Wysa, study + journalism + aggregators]
Confidence: HIGH that attachment drives return; two apps, published study theme "Humanness in AI is welcomed".

### Theme 4.2 — Loss/neglect hits as relational grief or guilt
- "Finch broke my heart I cried for days" — Finch reviewer after losing 2-year progress, Appbot 2026-08-24 [A]
- Guilt-on-missed-days and "deleted app after neglecting pet during hard week" claims appear only on SEO/spam-like pages (e.g., https://presentationbuilder.internationaltrucks.com/finch-self-care-pet-app) with no verifiable user quote — NOT counted.
- Counterpoint (press): "Finch doesn't take the guilt approach to goal-tracking, so you won't be scolded or shamed" — Engadget reviewer, date not captured, https://www.engadget.com/apps/this-self-care-virtual-pet-is-helping-me-get-my-act-together-160027169.html [J]
[distinct voices: 1 verified user on loss; guilt unverified]
Confidence: LOW for guilt-from-neglect specifically (Reddit blocked, the place it would live). Data-loss-as-heartbreak verified once.

### Theme 4.3 — "Silly / condescending" reaction to cute framing
- "I used to feel silly 'talking' to a cartoon penguin AI bot" — Wysa, App Store via justuseapp, date n/a [A]
- "most insulting onboarding" (re: dragging a yellow smiley to set mood; review titled "Condescending and inane") — Wysa, same page [A]

[distinct voices: 3]
Confidence: LOW-MEDIUM. Present but minority in retrieved samples; the silly-feeling reviewer says it passed. Note: condescension complaint targeted an infantilising INPUT mechanic, not the mascot itself (inference).
