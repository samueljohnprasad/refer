# ABC Analysis: research and decisions

Exercise: Ellis REBT. Activating event, Belief, Consequence, then test the belief and write a fairer one. Tracks feeling intensity before and after.
Brief self-authored. Design system: Happy (Nunito, sage on white, rim buttons), same shell as `thought-reframing.html`.

## Mobbin research (iOS)

| Pattern | Seen in | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Feeling chip picker, positive/negative groups | Bevel, Insight Timer, Fitbit | Reference only. The build uses thought reframing's own face cards, slot tray and 10 feelings (max 3) for consistency |
| Mood as one big numeral with a reacting face | Apple Health | Intensity gauge before and after, reusing the cloud and sun |
| Vertical timeline with lettered nodes and a connector | Shopify, Fly Delta, Trainline, Perplexity | Summary chain A, B, C |
| Free-text entry | Insight Timer journal | Text steps copy thought reframing: 4-row field, status line, "Need an example?" disclosure that fills the field |
| Before / after two-up | Apple Health, Opal | Summary weather pair and intensity scoreboard |

## Flow

intro, what happened (A), feelings (C), intensity, belief (B), test the belief (fact or guess, friend, helpful), fairer thought, intensity again, summary.
Feelings come before the belief because people find the feeling first and the thought second.

## Decisions

- Test step is three two-answer questions, not a free essay. Low effort when the mood is low.
- Summary copy is keyed to the result. A held-up belief or a stronger feeling is never framed as failure.
- Fairer thought: "true and kinder", not forced positivity.
- Belief step carries a mini A, B, C chain with B as a dashed gap that fills as the user types. The concept shows before the summary.
- "After" intensity starts at the "before" value, never pre-picked toward relief (same in fear ladder and behavioral activation).
- Summary leads with the fairer thought as the hero ("Next time it shows up, tell yourself").
- Conversation layer (see `DIRECTION.md`): the cloud asks in bubbles, answers come back as your bubbles, examples are quick replies, the three checks run one at a time on two big cards with dots.
