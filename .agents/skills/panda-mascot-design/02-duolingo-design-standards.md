## 2. The 6 Golden Rules of Duolingo Master Illustrators

### Rule 1: Geometric Primitive Construction
Duolingo characters are engineered from pure foundational geometry: capsules, circles, semi-circles, and crisp corner fillets. Zero erratic, wobbly bezier contours. Everything looks like it could be animated via vector bone rigs in Rive or Lottie.

### Rule 2: The 32px Squint Silhouette Test
If you fill the entire character silhouette in solid black and shrink it to a 32x32px notification icon, **the action must be instantly identifiable**. 
- A collapsed pancake reads as a flat horizontal blob.
- Proud tears reads as hands holding head with floating feet.
- Side-eye reads as crossed arms with angled head.

### Rule 3: Anatomy Simplification (Mitten Paws)
Zero individual fingers, knuckles, or claws. Paws are simple 1-piece rounded bean shapes. Arms articulate from the shoulder without complex elbows or musculature unless intentionally stylized (e.g. comic bicep flex).

### Rule 4: Color Economy & Hard Shadow Cuts
Ceiling of 3–4 primary solid fills and at most 1 hard-edge shadow tint per body part. Strictly no airbrushed radial gradients, digital painterly textures, or realistic surface fur.

### Rule 5: The Single-Token Narrative Rule
The physical body posture IS the story. If an accessory is required, it must be exactly ONE clean graphic token (e.g. workout headband, single teardrop, clipboard). Never paste multiple miscellaneous props (no cups + chairs + tables + plants).

### Rule 6: Psychological Mirroring (Lifecycle Fit)
Mascot is never decorative wallpaper. It serves as an active emotional mirror for the user's immediate CBT state:
- **Commitment / Start**: Determined posture, focused eyes, clipboard or headband.
- **Cognitive Trap / Distortion**: Skeptical deadpan side-eye, arms crossed.
- **Burnout / Acute Panic**: Deflated mochi pancake, zero judgment, comical relief.
- **Breakthrough / Lesson Complete**: Cheeks-clutched proud tears or star-eyed celebration.
- **Relapse / Drop-off Risk**: Pleading puppy-eyes, compassionate encouragement.
