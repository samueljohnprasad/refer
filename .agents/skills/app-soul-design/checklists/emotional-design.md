# Emotional-design checklist

Judgment checks. Record each as `[PASS]`/`[FAIL] ED#.#` in the critique with the reason.

## ED1 Being seen
- **ED1.1** The spec reflects the user's own words or choices back within one screen of entry on at least the check-in, thought catch and completion moments.
- **ED1.2** Validation precedes redirection: on low mood, the first response acknowledges ("That sounds heavy.") before any suggestion.
- **ED1.3** No response treats a distress entry as a success metric (no "+10 XP" on logging sadness).

## ED2 Relatedness without dependence
- **ED2.1** The companion stance is "beside", not "above": no lecturing, no grading the user's feelings.
- **ED2.2** The app points outward at least once per week of use (people, rest, the world) rather than positioning itself as the only support.
- **ED2.3** Autonomy: every reminder, memory and ritual is user-adjustable or skippable with no penalty copy.

## ED3 Rhythm and dose
- **ED3.1** Delight moments have numeric frequency caps and a decay rule.
- **ED3.2** Celebration amplitude scales with significance; a routine completion gets ≤ a subtle acknowledgement (≤600 ms, no confetti).
- **ED3.3** Every Duolingo/game-borrowed mechanic states its adaptation for mental health ("turned down: …").

## ED4 Continuity
- **ED4.1** The app remembers something from a previous session and surfaces it at least once in the first screen of a return visit.
- **ED4.2** Return after absence is framed as welcome, never as loss ("Good to see you" not "You lost your 12-day streak").

## ED5 Character (Phases 3, 4, 6)
- **ED5.1** Mascot expressions never mirror sadness back at the user to induce guilt; concern is shown as attention (gaze, lean), not crying.
- **ED5.2** Mascot word count per appearance ≤ the cap set in Phase 3; app copy carries instruction.
- **ED5.3** The mascot has defined absence zones and they include distress free-text and crisis resources.
- **ED5.4** Motion follows rossark principles: never teleport, calm curves, amplitudes within its recipe table, reduced motion = still.

## ED6 Warmth calibration
- **ED6.1** Copy passes the "would a kind, grounded adult friend say this?" read-aloud test; cloying markers (baby talk, "yay!!", excessive emoji, pet names) are absent.
- **ED6.2** Cultural range: copy avoids idioms and religious/cultural assumptions; tested against ≥2 locales in the repo if present.
