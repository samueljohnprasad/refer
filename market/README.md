## Executive Summary: Happy's Market Opportunity

1. **The Market Void**:
   * **Toy Apps (Finch)** own viral retention through pet mascots, but provide shallow therapeutic tools (no real CBT restructuring).
   * **Clinical Apps (MindShift)** have clinical validity, but suffer from sterile, text-heavy 2012 forms that users abandon in acute panic.
   * **Passive Audio (Calm, Headspace)** is saturated; users are fatigued by $70/yr subscriptions for 30-minute sleep stories.
2. **Happy's Wedge**:
   * **"Duolingo for CBT + Happi the Panda"**: Structured 3-minute interactive journeys, tactile 3D tools (Thought Catcher, Coping Cards), and multi-timeframe insights.
3. **First-Use Rule**:
   * Never paywall or force registration before value. The first cognitive reframe is always 100% free and completes within 90 seconds.
