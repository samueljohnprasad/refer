### Full Description (100% Clean ASCII - App Store Connect Validated)

```text
Most journaling apps ask you to stare at a blank page. Therapy worksheets often feel like homework. Happy takes a different approach: evidence-based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and daily journaling designed to feel simple, interactive, and rewarding - like Duolingo for your mind.

Talk out loud, write in your notebook, or tap through 5-minute interactive courses that help you understand your thoughts and break anxiety loops before they spiral.

WHAT YOU CAN DO IN HAPPY:

INTERACTIVE CBT COURSES
No dense textbook chapters. Move along visual paths that break down real CBT skills into 5-minute interactive exercises:
- Catch thinking traps like catastrophizing and black-and-white thinking.
- Learn practical frameworks for overthinking, social anxiety, and stress.
- Open milestone chests and earn XP as you complete each lesson.

TALK IT OUT OR SCAN YOUR NOTEBOOK
- Voice Journaling: When your mind is racing and you do not feel like typing, just speak. Happy transcribes your thoughts, highlights emotional triggers, and helps you reframe them.
- Paper Journal OCR: If you prefer pen and paper, snap a photo of your notebook page. Happy turns your handwritten notes into digital reflections you can search and track over time.

SEE YOUR PATTERNS OVER TIME
A single day does not tell the whole story. Happy connects your entries to show you what is actually happening:
- Daily Timelines: See what triggered stress or calm throughout the day.
- Weekly View: Notice patterns by day of the week and keep your streak moving.
- Monthly Insights: Learn which habits, sleep routines, or activities lift your mood or drain your energy.
- Yearly Reflection: Look back across 365 days of real personal progress.
- Focused Deep Dives: Dedicated breakdowns for Anxiety, Overthinking, and Mindfulness.

40+ INTERACTIVE COPING TOOLS
When anxiety spikes in the middle of a busy day, you need tools that work right away:
- Quick breath pacers (Box Breathing, 4-7-8 rhythm)
- Grounding exercises (5-4-3-2-1 technique)
- Socratic dialogue prompts to challenge worry spirals
- Decision trees to separate what you can control from what you cannot

A COMPANION THAT KEEPS YOU CONSISTENT
Building a habit is hard when life gets busy. A cheerful panda companion, gentle daily reminders, and streak milestones keep you motivated without feeling guilty.

PRIVATE AND YOURS
Everything you say, write, or scan stays private. We do not sell your data, and your entries are strictly yours.

Start calming your mind with tools you will actually look forward to using. Download Happy free today.
```
