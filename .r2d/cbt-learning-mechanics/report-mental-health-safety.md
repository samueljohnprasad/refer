# Mental-health safety — CBT learning mechanics

## Recommendation

Use **private mastery loop**.

Teach one small idea. Ask recall. Give specific correction. Retry changed case. Ask safe use-plan. Recheck later.

Advance from proof. Never from taps, time, XP, mood change, or forced disclosure.

Wellness adjunct only. Not diagnosis. Not treatment replacement. No social, streak loss, leaderboard, or FOMO.

Reversibility: two-way. Learning thresholds/content copy tunable. Medical-purpose claims: one-way regulatory risk.

## Confidence

82% for interaction + safety system.

High: privacy, claim boundary, choice/opt-out, structured CBT practice.

Medium: exact spacing days and mastery thresholds. Direct evidence for this exact wellness course stack absent.

## Evidence

Digital-mental-health specific:

- [MHRA DMHT qualification/classification](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/digital-mental-health-technology-qualification-and-classification): intended purpose plus functionality control medical-device boundary. Diagnosis, prevention, treatment, clinician-like claims raise regulation need.
- [NICE digital/mobile behaviour-change guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng183/chapter/recommendations): evidence standards, user-tailored goals, feedback/monitoring, informed data preferences; no dangerous goals.
- [NICE evidence standards framework](https://www.nice.org.uk/corporate/ecd7): digital health needs safety, quality, data-protection, credible evidence.
- [WHO mental-health policy guidance](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/380466/9789240106819-eng.pdf): digital support needs confidentiality, evidence base, accessibility, non-digital route; complements, not replaces, in-person support.
- [WHO mhGAP digital interventions](https://www.who.int/teams/mental-health-and-substance-use/treatment-care/mental-health-gap-action-programme/evidence-centre/self-harm-and-suicide/digital-interventions): even suicide-specific digital support only conditional, low-certainty; safety, consent, confidentiality, privacy needed.
- [dCBT-I RCT meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36966184/): digital CBT can affect measured outcomes in adult insomnia samples. **Do not transfer outcome claim to Happy wellness users.**

CBT / education evidence. Mostly clinical or general learning. Infer interaction mechanics. Do not infer treatment effect:

- [NICE adult depression guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/Recommendations): CBT links thoughts, beliefs, feelings, behaviour; structured, goal-oriented; uses coping skills and between-session practice. Clinical guidance, not wellness proof.
- [NICE computerised CBT guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg91/chapter/Recommendations): explain CBT model; encourage between-session tasks; challenge thoughts; monitor behaviour, thoughts, outcomes. Clinical/chronic-illness population.
- [Distributed + retrieval practice review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37615780/): 43/56 health-professions studies found spacing and/or retrieval benefit. General education inference.
- [CBT components RCT experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38536924/): cognitive restructuring changed a targeted negative self-attribution process. Clinical/experimental evidence; not a wellness outcome claim.
- [SAMHSA trauma-informed approach](https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/trauma-violence/trauma-informed-approaches-programs): safety, trust, collaboration, empowerment, voice/choice, resist retraumatization.
- [ICO special-category guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-are-the-rules-on-special-category-data/?q=dpa): health data needs greater protection and data minimisation.

## Key findings

- Completion proves exposure. It does not prove recall, correction, transfer, or retention.
- Closed, bounded answers can prove a taught micro-skill. Personal free text cannot safely prove truth, diagnosis, or cognitive distortion.
- CBT content needs active practice: link situation, thought, feeling, action; test alternatives; observe result; plan next step. NICE support this structure and practice. [NICE NG222](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/Recommendations), [NICE CG91](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg91/chapter/Recommendations)
- Space only successful retrieval. Start 1 day, 3 days, 10 days, 30 days. Miss = worked example + changed retry; next check in 1 day. Exact cadence is general-learning inference, not digital-CBT trial proof. [Retrieval/spacing review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37615780/)
- Distress, shame, low literacy, disability, privacy, and no-time are valid exits. Choice is safety system, not dropout defect. [SAMHSA](https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/trauma-violence/trauma-informed-approaches-programs), [WHO](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/380466/9789240106819-eng.pdf)

## Safe exercise / feedback patterns

| Rank | Format | CBT mapping | Feedback + retry | Proof / adapt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Changed-context scenario choice, then one-line why | Distortion identification; causal model | Say what clue fits. Show contrast: “This thought predicts worst case. We do not know outcome.” Retry new case. | Correct + reason in new case. Miss twice: show two visible examples, reduce one variable. |
| 2 | Evidence sort: facts / guesses / missing information | Evidence weighing | Confirm facts. Name uncertainty. Never say user thought is false. Retry by moving one card. | Correct sort in changed neutral scenario. |
| 3 | If–then plan with safe menu plus optional private text | Coping planning; behavioural experiment | Check plan has cue + small action + kind fallback. “Try, then notice. Result can differ.” No pass/fail on outcome. | Bounded plan fields complete. Later ask selected-action recall, not outcome compliance. |
| 4 | Prediction → small low-risk test → observe prompt | Behavioural experiment | “This tests one prediction. It does not prove worth, safety, or diagnosis.” Stop option always. | Learner identifies prediction, observation, learning. Never score real-world result. |
| 5 | Brief causal-chain ordering | Situation → thought → feeling → action / consequence | Explain one link. Retry changed case. | Correct relation, then transfer scenario. |
| 6 | Guided reflection / free text | Meaning-making | Reflect, offer two examples, skip. No automated truth judgement, diagnosis, distortion label, or mastery credit. | Completion only; excluded from learning proof and analytics. |

Use simple words: “fact,” “guess,” “test,” “next small step,” “not sure yet.”

Use neutral scenarios first. Offer everyday, work, relationship, health, and “use a sample only” paths. Never require learner trauma, abuse, grief, identity, sex, violence, or self-harm details.

## Progression system

Skill state per micro-skill:

`introduced -> supported recall -> independent recall -> changed-case transfer -> delayed retained`

Unlock next skill after independent recall + changed-case transfer. Award calm progress label: “You can use this in a new situation.” No XP for opening, tapping, or writing.

Show one finite next review. Let learner snooze, change reminder, or turn reviews off. No loss language.

Support-up:

- First miss: clue.
- Second miss: worked contrast + two adaptive examples.
- Third miss or “too hard”: pause, simpler sibling item, or leave. Never loop shamefully.

Support-down:

- Two independent correct changed cases: remove clue; change context.
- Delayed correct: mark retained; extend interval.

## Failure / opt-out rules

- Always show: `Skip for now`, `Use sample`, `Choose easier example`, `Stop this exercise`.
- Skip changes no mastery down, no XP loss, no streak loss, no warning.
- Never force exposure, body focus, breathing, memory recall, social disclosure, or behavioural experiment.
- Before experiment: block illegal, dangerous, medical, financial, relationship-confrontation, self-harm, substance, and trauma-related tasks. Offer safe tiny alternative.
- Distress path: “You do not need to continue. Pause. Contact someone you trust or local urgent support if you may be unsafe.” Provide locale-aware crisis/help route only after explicit risk content or user asks. Do not make learner answer risk questions to exit.
- No crisis detection from course errors, mood rating, silence, or free text. Such inference needs separate safety design, human-review operations, and legal review.

## Claim-language guardrails

Say:

- “Learn a CBT-style skill for everyday stress.”
- “This may help you notice patterns and try a small step.”
- “Not medical advice. Not a diagnosis. Not a replacement for professional care.”
- “A score shows performance on these course examples.”

Do not say:

- “You have [condition].”
- “This thought is irrational / wrong.”
- “This will treat, prevent, diagnose, cure, assess severity, predict risk, or replace therapy.”
- “Your score means you are better / recovered / safe.”

Reason: intended-purpose wording and function affect digital mental-health regulatory boundary. [MHRA](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/digital-mental-health-technology-qualification-and-classification), [NICE ESF](https://www.nice.org.uk/corporate/ecd7)

## Telemetry privacy boundaries

Capture only event metadata for bounded course items:

`course_version, node_id, skill_id, item_id, item_version, event, correctness, attempt_number, hint_tier, support_level, time_bucket, review_interval, mastery_state, skip_type`

Never capture in PostHog or any analytics:

- journal, free text, voice, AI prompt/output, personal situation, names, contacts, scenario customisation, raw answer payload, mood/symptom explanation, or crisis language.

Do not send item text if it can carry sensitive context. Keep analytics IDs server-side. Coarsen time to buckets. Default no cross-product profiling, ads, social comparison, or ranking.

Persist bounded answer correctness only for learner-facing mastery. Encrypt/access-control. Give delete/export controls. Keep retention short and documented. Health-adjacent data needs minimisation and higher protection. [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-are-the-rules-on-special-category-data/?q=dpa), [NICE ESF](https://www.nice.org.uk/corporate/ecd7)

Measure learning, not wellbeing claim:

- immediate independent recall rate;
- changed-case transfer rate;
- delayed recall rate;
- hint-to-independent conversion;
- skip/stop rate by item version;
- harmful-item report rate;
- completion separately.

Self-report mood remains optional reflection. Never use it as diagnosis, mastery, safety clearance, or efficacy proof. Digital self-report formats can be reliable for some instruments, but reliability varies; validated measures and clinical context still matter. [Systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25472463/), [NICE NG222](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/Recommendations)

## Contradictions

- Digital CBT has clinical outcome evidence. Happy serves general wellness adults. Pick skill-performance claims only; no symptom/treatment claim. [dCBT-I meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36966184/), [MHRA](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/digital-mental-health-technology-qualification-and-classification)
- Treatment guidance uses practitioner review. Happy is self-guided. Copy active-practice mechanics; do not copy clinical monitoring or claim equivalent care. [NICE CG91](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg91/chapter/Recommendations), [WHO](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/380466/9789240106819-eng.pdf)
- More measurement can adapt learning. More sensitive collection increases harm/privacy risk. Pick bounded correctness plus explicit opt-in reflection; never inspect content. [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-are-the-rules-on-special-category-data/?q=dpa), [NICE NG183](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng183/chapter/recommendations)

## Open questions

- Which locales can offer verified crisis/help routes? Need owner, review cadence, escalation policy.
- Who clinically reviews items, harmful-item reports, claim copy, and experiment safety tags? Need named accountable role.
- Which valid, non-diagnostic learner-outcome measure can supplement skill telemetry? Pilot only. No efficacy claim before study.
- What retention/delete window meets actual jurisdiction and Supabase/PostHog data flow? Need privacy/legal review.
- What threshold proves each micro-skill without punishing language, disability, or language difference? Pilot with diverse general-adult users.

## Rejected alternatives

- Done-button / XP completion. Measures activity only.
- Mood improvement as mastery. Self-report is not skill proof or diagnosis.
- AI grading free text. Privacy, hallucination, shame, false authority.
- Forced personal disclosure / trauma narrative. Retraumatization risk. No need for skill proof.
- Generic “Correct!” or “Wrong.” No corrective mechanism.
- Infinite retry, streaks, leaderboard, social feed, FOMO. Pressure replaces learning and privacy.
- Auto-escalate after repeated wrong answers. Wrong answer is learning signal, not clinical risk signal.
