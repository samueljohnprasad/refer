# Happy — US CPP Campaign Plan

Launch plan for approved Custom Product Pages. The goal is qualified downloads first, then subscription learning without making unsupported clinical promises.

## Campaign map

| Campaign | Audience intent | CPP | Initial budget share | Primary CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| CBT Journeys | CBT, thought reframing, guided exercises | CBT Journeys | 40% | Start a guided practice |
| Anxiety Tools | anxiety relief, grounding, coping tools | Anxiety Tools | 35% | Find a tool for this moment |
| Voice Journal | voice journal, talk therapy journal, spoken reflection | Voice Journal | 25% | Speak your thoughts freely |

Use the same split for Apple Search Ads and paid social only if both channels are active. If budget is very small, launch CBT Journeys and Anxiety Tools first; keep Voice Journal as the third test.

## Apple Search Ads setup

Create one campaign per angle with separate ad groups. Match the ad group keyword theme to the CPP message.

### Recommended campaign layers

Use the creator’s competitor-intercept idea as one layer of the account, not the whole account:

1. **Brand:** Happy and close variants, protected with a low controlled bid.
2. **Category:** CBT, anxiety tools, thought reframing, voice journal, and related intent.
3. **Competitor intercept:** exact-match competitor names and competitor-plus-intent phrases.
4. **Discovery:** broad match and Search Match with a small capped budget to find new queries.

Apple supports exact and broad match separately, and recommends discovery campaigns for mining new search terms. Search Match can uncover demand, but turning it off everywhere would remove that learning loop. Keep discovery isolated so it cannot consume the core campaign budget. [Apple keyword guidance](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/best-practices/keywords)

For competitor campaigns, bid on the term without using the competitor’s name in the ad copy, screenshot text, or promotional claims. Review each term for relevance and pause terms that bring low-quality intent. Apple requires ad claims to be substantiated and ads not to mislead users. [Apple Ads policies](https://ads.apple.com/policies)

### Initial bid and audience test

- Start with a conservative Max CPT bid around the current account’s expected range; do not assume `$1.20` is right for every keyword.
- Give each ad group 48 hours to spend before increasing bids.
- If an ad group has no delivery, raise the bid in small steps rather than jumping to a high bid.
- Start with new users and iPhone-only if that matches the available product experience.
- Keep demographics broad initially. Highly restrictive audience settings can reduce reach, especially for users with Personalized Ads turned off. [Apple ad variation guidance](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/best-practices/ad-variations)
- Keep ad scheduling off until there is enough hourly data to justify a schedule.

### Territory rollout

Start with US only. Add the other English-speaking markets after the US campaign has enough data to establish product-page conversion, install quality, and subscription behavior. Use separate campaigns by country so bids, currency, and CPP performance remain readable.

Suggested order:

1. US
2. UK, CA, AU
3. Other localized markets after localized metadata and pricing are verified

Do not treat Tier 2 or Tier 3 bids as automatically cheaper or more profitable. Test them only after the US baseline exists, and judge them on paid conversion and retention rather than CPI alone.

### CBT Journeys

Suggested ad groups:

- `cbt-core`: cbt journal, cbt app, cognitive behavioral therapy app
- `thought-reframing`: thought reframing, negative thoughts, change thinking patterns
- `guided-journal`: guided journal, mental health journal prompts, therapeutic journal

CPP: `CBT Journeys`

### Anxiety Tools

Suggested ad groups:

- `anxiety-tools`: anxiety tools, anxiety help app, manage anxious thoughts
- `grounding`: grounding exercises, breathing exercise anxiety, coping skills
- `overthinking`: stop overthinking, racing thoughts help, calming exercises

CPP: `Anxiety Tools`

### Voice Journal

Suggested ad groups:

- `voice-journal`: voice journal, audio diary, voice notes journal
- `talk-it-out`: talk through feelings, spoken reflection, journal by voice

CPP: `Voice Journal`

Start with exact match for the cleanest signal. Add broad match only after search-term review. Add negative keywords for meditation, music, therapy provider, crisis, and unrelated medical intent where appropriate.

## Subscription funnel

Track each stage by campaign and CPP:

1. Product-page view
2. Install
3. Onboarding completed
4. First exercise completed
5. Paywall viewed
6. Trial started
7. Subscription purchased
8. Day-7 return

Do not optimize for downloads alone. A campaign is useful only when the acquired users reach a first exercise and show healthy subscription or retention behavior.

## First 14-day operating plan

### Days 1–3: quality check

- Confirm each ad group opens the intended CPP URL.
- Confirm the app opens, onboarding completes, and the first exercise is reachable.
- Check that paywall and purchase events are recorded.
- Pause search terms that are clearly unrelated or crisis-oriented.

### Days 4–7: creative and keyword cleanup

- Review search terms and move promising terms into exact match.
- Compare product-page conversion by CPP.
- Check onboarding completion and first-exercise completion by campaign.
- Keep copy stable while the first signal accumulates.

### Days 8–14: subscription read

- Compare paywall view rate, trial start rate, purchase rate, and Day-7 return.
- Shift up to 20% of budget toward the strongest qualified campaign.
- Do not declare a winner from a tiny sample; use at least 1,000 product-page views per CPP before a firm conversion decision.

## Campaign naming

Use a consistent naming scheme:

`US__<angle>__<channel>__<match-type>__v1`

Examples:

- `US__CBT-Journeys__ASA__exact__v1`
- `US__Anxiety-Tools__META__prospecting__v1`
- `US__Voice-Journal__ASA__exact__v1`

## Decision rules

- If a CPP has strong page conversion but weak onboarding, fix the post-install experience before increasing spend.
- If downloads are strong but paywall views are weak, improve activation and feature discovery rather than making the ad more aggressive.
- If trial starts are strong but purchases are weak, inspect paywall clarity, pricing presentation, and trial expectations.
- If one campaign produces low-quality or crisis-oriented intent, pause it even if its install cost looks attractive.

## What to create in the ad platforms

- Three ASA campaigns with the matching CPP assigned to each ad group.
- One or two social campaigns using the same three CPP destinations.
- A simple weekly report with spend, impressions, product-page views, installs, onboarding completion, first exercise completion, paywall views, trials, purchases, and Day-7 return.
