# Happy Apple Search Ads Launch Plan

## Objective

Acquire people looking for a practical CBT journaling or coping tool, then optimize toward activated subscribers rather than the lowest-cost install.

Start with US Search Results campaigns only. Add Search tab, product-page, or competitor campaigns after the core intent campaigns have enough paid-conversion data.

## Readiness Gates

Do not create or fund a campaign until all of these are true:

- The `1.5.3` metadata package is approved for the released version.
- The first three default screenshots are exported from real Happy UI.
- Each linked Custom Product Page is approved and visible, with its matching screenshots and promotional text.
- The trial duration, renewal price, billing period, restore path, and legal links are confirmed from the active offering.
- App Analytics and purchase events are checked with a real test run.

## Campaign Structure

| Campaign | Match | Destination | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| US — Brand | Exact | Default product page | Protect people already looking for Happy. |
| US — CBT Journeys | Exact | CBT Journeys Custom Product Page | Capture people seeking CBT exercises and thought-reframing practice. |
| US — Anxiety Tools | Exact | Anxiety Tools Custom Product Page | Capture people seeking a guided coping exercise. |
| US — Voice Journal | Exact | Voice Journal Custom Product Page | Capture people who prefer talking through a reflection. |
| US — Discovery | Search Match only | Default product page | Find new search terms without contaminating intent campaigns. |

Keep each campaign in its own budget and reporting view. Do not mix brand, discovery, and category intent; their conversion rates answer different questions.

## Exclusions

Add these as negative keywords at launch and review the search terms report weekly:

- crisis
- suicide
- self harm
- emergency
- hotline
- medication
- psychiatrist
- diagnosis
- therapist
- jobs
- certification
- worksheet pdf

These searches indicate urgent care, clinical-provider, employment, education, or document intent that Happy should not monetize.

## Budget and Bids

Do not set a fixed spend or bid from this document. Use Apple Ads’ current suggested bid range as the starting reference, then cap each campaign to an amount you are comfortable treating as learning spend.

- Give each exact-intent campaign its own daily cap.
- Keep Discovery lower than exact-intent campaigns.
- Increase a bid only when a keyword produces qualified activation and trial or purchase events.
- Pause a keyword when it attracts irrelevant terms, does not activate users, or harms paid-conversion efficiency.

## Measurement

| Layer | Primary metric | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad | Tap-through rate | Search term matches the product-page promise. |
| Store | Product-page conversion | First three screenshots communicate the right job. |
| Activation | Onboarding completion and first exercise completion | Campaign traffic reaches an initial win. |
| Monetization | Paywall view, trial start, purchase | Premium is offered after enough value is understood. |
| Quality | Day-1 return, Day-7 return, trial-to-paid | Spend does not buy short-lived installs. |

Do not declare a winning keyword using installs alone. Compare downstream activation and paid conversion by campaign, keyword cluster, and Custom Product Page.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

1. Review search terms and move relevant, converting terms from Discovery into the matching exact-intent campaign.
2. Add irrelevant or unsafe queries as negatives.
3. Compare product-page conversion by Custom Product Page.
4. Compare first exercise, trial, purchase, and Day-7 return by source.
5. Scale only the keyword clusters that retain qualified users and subscribers.

## First Experiment

**Hypothesis:** People searching for CBT exercises will convert better when the product page starts with the guided journey and thought-reframing story than when they see a generic feature list.

**Control:** Default page with the approved first-three screenshot sequence.

**Campaign page:** CBT Journeys Custom Product Page with the same core sequence, but all later screenshots focused on guided progression and thought reframing.

**Decision:** Keep the page only if it improves product-page conversion and does not reduce onboarding completion, first exercise completion, trial start, or paid conversion.

## Apple Platform Notes

- Apple supports Custom Product Pages in Search Results ads and lets each page use different screenshots, promotional text, and keyword targeting.
- Use a unique keyword set for each Custom Product Page so its promise matches the person’s search.
- Custom Product Pages must be approved before users can see them.
- Apple Ads supports both targeting and negative keywords; use both to preserve relevance.
