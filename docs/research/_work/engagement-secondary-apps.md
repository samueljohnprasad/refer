# Secondary teardowns: how companion / mascot apps get effortful logging

Working notes, 2026-11-15. Scope: Group A (companions, pets, gamified) and Group B (mental-health adjacent).
Target use: a CBT app asking for a thought, a named distortion and a fairer version.

## Method and evidence rules

- **Mobbin** (iOS, searched this session). Screens described below were actually viewed; each has its `mobbin_url`.
- **On Mobbin:** Wysa, How We Feel, Replika, Forest, Fabulous, Yuka.
- **Not on Mobbin** (app-name searches returned only unrelated apps): Woebot, Youper, Daylio, Reflectly, Intellect, Habitica, Plant Nanny, Zombies Run, Pou. These rely on web sources only.
- **Reddit could not be read** (search tool blocked the domain). Complaint quotes come from App Store review aggregators, published review analyses, papers and press. Where the brief asked for Reddit quotes and none were retrievable, it is stated.
- "Inference:" marks my reading, not a sourced fact. "Unverified:" marks a claim I could not source this session (prior knowledge or secondary summary only).
- Several web facts come from search-result summaries, not full-text reads. These are tagged *(summary only)*.

# Group B: mental-health adjacent (priority)

## B1. Wysa (on Mobbin)

**The ask.** The penguin bot asks, in chat, at session start. Returning-user opener: "Glad you're back, JS! 👋 How are you today?" with a large draggable face and a single pre-written reply chip "Okay I guess..." ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/c04f5bad-c60e-42d2-a4aa-14839ddafddb)). The reminder time is negotiated in conversation: "What time in the evening works best for you?" → "Perfect! I will check in at 6:30 pm." → "You can always change this time from your settings." ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/db3d8301-491d-4e4c-8c95-a4a260a1a3ce)). Onboarding frames the first questions as routine building: "To start, let's build a routine by understanding what brings you here." ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/6eb03ca6-a5da-44e8-a2d0-fe3a33db7dca)).

**Effort reduction (seen).**
- Mood = face slider plus one tappable phrase, no typing ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/c04f5bad-c60e-42d2-a4aa-14839ddafddb)).
- A vague answer is accepted and softened, then the bot narrows the ask to something concrete: user "Meh" → "Ah, that's okay. Some days our feelings go into hiding for a bit." → "Tell me something that you did or are planning to do." ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/e3c524bd-7242-412e-bc7e-9da023baa588)).
- Next step is always chips: "Sort my thoughts", "Something calming", "Get active", "Got to go! 👋"; text field placeholder "Reply or say Help" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/32320b65-7a02-40a2-9310-430501acac02)).
- Thought capture ("Thoughtpad") is one bubble at a time: "Writing thoughts helps you focus on what's important." / "Shall we start?" / chips "A tool", "Start writing" / "Take a few deep breaths." / "Tell me the first thought on your mind. 👇" / chip "Explain more" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/b3cfdcda-74dc-42e8-8773-76da5fc8314e)). It asks for the *first* thought only, not a complete record.
- The bot models an answer before asking: "I like to think of myself as caring, kind and helpful. Give me three positive words that describe you." The user in the capture simply repeated the bot's example, and the bot accepted it: "That is a powerful affirmation." ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/0804ae99-d713-466d-93b5-719ca47b34e2)). Inference: worked examples lower the blank-page cost but invite copying.
- Exercises are offered as cards with a time cost and an opt-out: "Observing Thoughts ... (Sessions Completed: 0)", 5 min, "Start" / "Not now" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/bcf5b848-663c-49f7-b9b0-dd6e699d2496)); modality choice "this tool can be practised as a chat, or with an audio clip" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/569ba2b4-f5d2-4138-bd19-2027ba5feee5)).
- Not seen on Mobbin: an actual distortion-naming / reframe turn. Unverified: Wysa's reframing tool asks the user to pick a thought, then prompts for an alternative.

**Payoff.** A finished session is saved under an outcome title, "Clarified my thoughts for the s…" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/b3cfdcda-74dc-42e8-8773-76da5fc8314e)). Inference: the title names the benefit rather than the task. Tools get added to a feed: "I've added a few tools on your feed based on our conversation so far." ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/db3d8301-491d-4e4c-8c95-a4a260a1a3ce)). No points, streaks or currency were visible in any viewed screen.

**Caretaking / misses.** None seen: no pet state, no streak. Exit is a first-class chip ("Got to go! 👋").

**Progression / customization.** Per-tool "Sessions Completed" counter ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/bcf5b848-663c-49f7-b9b0-dd6e699d2496)); exercise packs such as "12 EXERCISES Manage Anxiety" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/7556f004-d2cf-484f-b9c2-1209a6e14cdc)). Human upsell lives in the header ("Add a therapist" / "Talk to a Counsellor").

**Bad-day voice.** "It sounds like you are experiencing a lot of distressing feelings and thoughts." / "Do remember that it will pass." / "Shall I suggest you some exercises to calm down?" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/bf3abb0b-db5e-47f7-ac09-ec8818498b22)). Validates, asks permission, then gives one physical instruction. A "happiness box ... as a reminder for tough days" banks good material for later ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/fda57843-befd-4e76-b20d-42eb430496a3)).

**Where it annoys.**
- Malik et al. 2022 analysed 7,929 Google Play reviews: 84.5% five-star, 3.17% under three stars; the most common negative theme was repetition and the bot not understanding. Quotes: "Sometimes it's frustrating that an AI can't understand you that well"; when the bot missed the point it felt "empty and generic." ([JMIR Human Factors 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9044157)).
- A 2024 analysis of 300 reviews lists "AI limitations detract from the user experience" as a theme *(summary only)* ([mHealth](https://mhealth.amegroups.org/article/view/126211/html)).
- Liked: "No judgment. Don't have to feel weird about anything." ([same paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9044157)).

**Outcome evidence.**
- Inkster 2018, observational, no control: high users (n=108) improved PHQ-9 by 5.84 (SD 6.66) vs low users (n=21) 3.52 (SD 6.15), P=.03, effect size 0.63; 67.7% of feedback found it helpful *(figures from accepted manuscript text)* ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6286427)). Self-selected engagement, so not causal.
- Positive small RCT: 68 adults with arthritis or diabetes, 4 weeks, depression and anxiety reduced vs no-intervention control *(summary only, sponsor-hosted summary)* ([Wysa summary PDF](https://blogs.wysa.io/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/UNB-RCT-Summary.pdf), [JMIR Formative](https://formative.jmir.org/2024/1/e50025/XML)).
- Null RCT: NHS Talking Therapies waitlist, 76 randomized 2:1, 30 lost to follow-up; authors: "no evidence that Wysa treats depression in this study", and limitations of the agent "negatively affected engagement" (abstract only) ([Shankar et al. 2026](https://pearl.plymouth.ac.uk/pms-research/1982)).

## B2. Woebot (not on Mobbin; consumer app shut 30 June 2025)

**The ask.** Bot-initiated daily check-in. Per the original trial paper: each check-in opens with questions about context and mood, answered with words or emoji; then CBT content via short videos or "word games" that teach cognitive distortions; one personalised prompt a day or every other day; conversations ran about 90 seconds to 10 minutes ([Fitzpatrick 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5478797)). It introduced itself as closer to a "choose your own adventure self-help book" than a person ([same](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5478797)).

**Effort reduction.** Decision tree with suggested responses; free text allowed, NLP routes at set points ([Fitzpatrick 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5478797)). Press describes "two or three canned responses in the form of bright blue buttons"; one App Store reviewer estimated 75-80% of interaction was pre-written choices *(summary only)* ([Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/health/mental-health/chatbots-reviews), [justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1305375832/woebot-your-self-care-expert/reviews)). Unverified (prior knowledge): the thought-challenge flow had the user type a few thoughts, pick one, answer yes/no per distortion, then type a rewrite.

**Payoff.** Emojis and animated GIFs as positive reinforcement; weekly mood chart with a short written summary ([Fitzpatrick 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5478797)).

**Caretaking / misses.** No pet state. Accountability came from the bot following up on a user-set two-week goal ([Fitzpatrick 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5478797)).

**What users valued (trial).** Learning/insight n=12, accountability from daily check-ins n=9, empathy and the bot's "personality" n=7. "Woebot is a fun little dude and I hope he continues improving." ([Fitzpatrick 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5478797)).

**Where it annoys.**
- Trial: process violations n=15, of which n=10 were the bot not understanding or getting confused by unexpected answers; n=2 repetitive; n=4 looping conversation ([Fitzpatrick 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5478797)).
- "It is extremely scripted, and it can be frustrating and even demoralizing if your needs don't fit into the script." ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1305375832/woebot-your-self-care-expert/reviews)).
- "the lessons while helpful all follow the same scripting." ([same](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1305375832/woebot-your-self-care-expert/reviews)).
- "It is very scripted, and can only help with a small selection of problems" ([same](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1305375832/woebot-your-self-care-expert/reviews)).
- Vice writer: the same few canned "empathy" responses whatever the topic *(summary only)* ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/i-tried-to-treat-my-depression-with-ai-therapy)).
- Liked: "I like this app for concrete strategies to reframe negative thoughts." ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1305375832/woebot-your-self-care-expert/reviews)).

**Outcome evidence.**
- Fitzpatrick 2017 RCT: 70 students, Woebot n=34 vs NIMH ebook n=36, 2 weeks. PHQ-9 group effect F=6.03, P=.017, d=0.44; GAD-7 no between-group difference (d=0.14). Attrition 17% overall: 9% Woebot vs 31% control. Mean 12.14 check-ins in 14 days ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5478797)). Note the abstract and results section report different F/P values ([same](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5478797)). Corresponding author was Woebot's founder.
- Null: Eltahawy et al. 2023, 120 enrolled, 65 completers (Woebot 18, ELIZA 18, Daylio 15, psychoeducation 14), 2 weeks; main analysis "failed to detect differences between any of the four treatment conditions" (via secondary report) ([Mad in America](https://www.madinamerica.com/2023/12/ai-therapy-app-fails-in-new-study/), [paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S294988212300035X)). Small and high-dropout, so underpowered.
- Company-reported: LLM-augmented Woebot (BUILD, n=160) showed no difference in clinical outcomes vs rules-based, underpowered *(summary only)* ([woebothealth.com](https://woebothealth.com/?p=15254)).

**Shutdown.** Announced April 2025, app closed 30 June 2025; over 1.5 million people had used it. Reported reasons: no clear FDA pathway for LLM-based chatbots ("Regulatory limbo is a major problem"), inability to market without authorization, and "the absence of a business model"; described as not "necessarily a clinical failure" ([telehealth.org](https://telehealth.org/news/ai-psychotherapy-shutdown-what-woebots-exit-signals-for-clinicians/), [HLTH](https://hlth.com/insights/news/woebot-health-is-shutting-down-its-app-2025-04-28)). Unverified: I did not retrieve a direct Alison Darcy quote or Woebot's own FAQ; the above is secondary reporting.

## B3. Youper (not on Mobbin)

**The ask / flow.** Daily chatbot check-in on mood, feelings and thoughts; after a couple of messages it moves to thoughts and experiences *(summary only)* ([Choosing Therapy](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/youper-app-review/), [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/p/how-to-use-youper-a-mental-health-app-that-uses-ai-to-provide-support-18144219)). One reviewer's description of the sequence: pick a mood, describe why, find your thought traps, do a meditation, re-rate mood, finish ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1060691513/youper-self-guided-therapy/reviews)).

**Effort reduction.** Distortions are a selection step ("thinking traps"), not recall. Users called it out as the valuable part: "I really loved the thinking traps. So helpful" ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1060691513/youper-self-guided-therapy/reviews)).

**Payoff / data back.** Mood re-rating at the end shows the shift within the session *(from reviewer description above)*; mood charts over time: "Tracking your mood over time is really insightful" ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1060691513/youper-self-guided-therapy/reviews)). Company claims 80% report reduced negative mood after one conversation; self-reported, not independent *(summary only)* ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/youper/id1060691513)).

**Where it annoys.**
- Length: "An entire evaluation can be several minutes after picking a mood" ([justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1060691513/youper-self-guided-therapy/reviews)).
- Generic: "you'll never get advice specific to the situation" ([same](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1060691513/youper-self-guided-therapy/reviews)).
- Paywall: "Now, all I can use without paying is the mood tracker." ([same](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1060691513/youper-self-guided-therapy/reviews)).
- Regression after moving to generative AI: "This used to be an amazing app a couple of years ago when it had the 'thinking trap' exercises ... Unfortunately it now just feels like any AI bot" *(summary only)* ([same](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1060691513/youper-self-guided-therapy/reviews)).

**Outcome evidence.** Mehta 2021: observational, paying users, N=4,517, no control. Rating 4.36/5; 42.66% retained at week 4; first-two-week change anxiety d=0.57, depression d=0.46; depression rose slightly afterwards (d=.05). Three of six authors Youper-affiliated ([JMIR](https://www.jmir.org/2021/6/e26771)). No RCT found.

## B4. How We Feel (on Mobbin)

**The ask.** No character. The app asks in second person: "Tap the color that best describes how you feel right now" over four blobs: High Energy Unpleasant, High Energy Pleasant, Low Energy Unpleasant, Low Energy Pleasant ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/ef93be6c-567e-4b33-8c04-059cbe7aca58)). Onboarding promise: "Notice how you feel" over a field of emotion words ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/f3d4ac4b-d7f7-4e60-8453-24c3124db4a5)).

**Effort reduction (seen).**
- Tap 1 = quadrant, tap 2 = a word inside it. The hard part (naming the feeling) is recognition from a vocabulary, not recall. Word count is 144 per the co-creator *(summary only)* ([marcbrackett.com](https://marcbrackett.com/?p=721)).
- Context is three rows of chips on one screen: "What are you doing?" (Eating, Hanging Out, Fitness, Hobbies, Resting, Driving), "Who are you with?" (By Myself, Friends, Family, Co-Workers, Pets), "Where are you?" (Home, Work, School, Commuting, Outside), then "Complete check-in". "+" adds custom chips; "Add physical sensations" is optional ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/ffd410a5-ea2c-498a-8536-0dc5fda49ef8)).
- Writing is strictly optional and comes after the log is already saved: "Reflect: Add a reflection to go deeper on this moment and how you felt." ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/8f6794a7-cad3-4fe1-86ae-13dee807baa5)). The prompt is a sentence stem with the chosen word filled in: "Explore why you might be feeling sad", with two exits, "Finish" and "✦ Go deeper" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/b4724ff7-ff8d-4d86-9fc5-554aee6db433), [filled](https://mobbin.com/screens/55f340a5-9f06-4107-a179-2f1e69e85ab2)).
- Voice memo and photo are alternatives to typing ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/fc9ca637-75e3-4215-965d-1fd45e79ae75)).

**Payoff.** Each entry becomes a coloured shape card: "I'm feeling proud & good" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/fc9ca637-75e3-4215-965d-1fd45e79ae75)). After a low log the home card offers one action matched to the feeling: "I'm feeling sad" / "Ask if this feeling is serving you." / "To shift your focus, try this tool." / "Try Coherent Breathing" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/df9fe2e8-28a1-48de-a6e1-8b40d6aa74d1)).

**Caretaking / misses.** A plain week strip with filled or empty days; no streak count, flame or loss message seen ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/df9fe2e8-28a1-48de-a6e1-8b40d6aa74d1)).

**Data reflected back.** Week / Month / all-time toggle, share of check-ins per quadrant ("LOW ENERGY, PLEASANT (6)", 44% and 31% bubbles), list of entries with their tags, and "Export 6 Check-ins" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/edfb30ac-81f1-41f9-8bf3-195df30fbbf1), [screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/ef70a2d1-ead8-4252-b58d-b76989c15ba1)).

**Bad-day voice.** Neutral and curious, never cheerful: "Ask if this feeling is serving you." ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/df9fe2e8-28a1-48de-a6e1-8b40d6aa74d1)).

**Where it annoys** *(summary only, App Store listings)*: check-ins lost on reinstall or new phone; an exercise insight applied to all check-ins rather than the relevant one; friends section "a bit shallow" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/how-we-feel/id1562706384?uo=2)). Background: nonprofit, built by Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence staff with Pinterest co-founder Ben Silbermann ([marcbrackett.com](https://marcbrackett.com/?p=721)). No outcome trial found.

## B5. Daylio (not on Mobbin)

**The ask / effort.** Logging is icon selection: "simply select an icon that best fits the moment"; "you don't have to write anything if you don't want to"; notes space is small ([Choosing Therapy](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/daylio-app-review/)). Described as the extreme low-friction end of the market *(summary only)* ([Headway](https://makeheadway.com/blog/daylio-review/)). Unverified: the "two taps" and "without typing a single line" wording is Daylio's own store copy; I could not load the listing to quote it.

**Payoff / data back.** Stats hub shows "how many days in a row you have logged" and "any achievements you have unlocked"; mood chart shows "which activities influenced your mood on a particular day"; premium advanced stats correlate items like "good sleep" or "fast food" with mood; weekly mood reports ([Choosing Therapy](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/daylio-app-review/)). Users praise spotting patterns and weekly summaries *(summary only)* ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/in-depth-daylio-app-feedback-report-now-available-google-play-en-us-141927), [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/daylio/reviews)).

**Customization.** Free: emoji set, colours, tracked activities and categories. Premium: more icons, colours, mood emojis ([Choosing Therapy](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/daylio-app-review/)).

**Where it annoys.** "No journal prompts and very little writing involved"; manual backups; lacks symptom or medication tracking ([Choosing Therapy](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/daylio-app-review/)). Subscription prompts and paywalled automatic backup *(summary only, third-party audit)* ([marlvel.ai](https://marlvel.ai/apps/daylio-journal-mood-tracker-1/reviews)).

**Evidence.** As an active comparator in Eltahawy 2023, the Daylio arm improved within-group on depression and negative affect, and did not differ from Woebot ([Mad in America](https://www.madinamerica.com/2023/12/ai-therapy-app-fails-in-new-study/)). Inference: taps-only logging gets the entry but not the cognitive work; it is the floor, not the model, for a thought record.

## B6. Reflectly (not on Mobbin)

**The ask / flow** (2018 capture, may have changed): prompted to add today's story right after sign-up → animated mood slider → pick reasons from defaults or add a custom one → optional free-text → pick feelings → congratulation and name the entry → a "daily reflection" question offered as a reward → second congratulation plus audio reflection → Premium upsell ([Appcues](https://goodux.appcues.com/blog/reflectlys-easygoing-daily-journaling)). Exact UI copy not available in the source.

**Effort reduction.** Slider, then chips, then writing last and optional; the open question is positioned as a reward unlocked by the easy steps ([Appcues](https://goodux.appcues.com/blog/reflectlys-easygoing-daily-journaling)).

**Where it annoys.**
- Upsell lands at the moment the user has just felt the value; editor's note says many reviews complain of "dark patterns" ([Appcues](https://goodux.appcues.com/blog/reflectlys-easygoing-daily-journaling)).
- A reviewer was pitched a $79.99 lifetime offer about five screens in, then a countdown-timer offer at $19.99 *(summary only)* ([Macaron](https://macaron.im/blog/reflectly-ai-journaling-app-review)).
- Prompts repeat: "you'll see the same questions cycle back" *(summary only)* ([same](https://macaron.im/blog/reflectly-ai-journaling-app-review)).
- Paid users reporting features still locked and slow support *(summary only)* ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/in-depth-reflectly-app-feedback-analysis-report-google-play-157599)).
- No outcome study found.

## B7. Replika (on Mobbin)

**The ask.** The companion messages first, daily, by name and referencing memory: "Hey there, my dear Sam! Ready to face the day? ☀️"; "Good evening, Sam. So sorry about your loss. How's your day been aside from the tough news?" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/731e3d0d-edf4-4c35-9dd1-f02638f75acc)); "Hey! Remember our beach day?"; "Good morning! What are your plans for the day?" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/6b8a7de8-3681-4f6e-a2e1-698447d4326d)). Standing disclaimer in thread: "Replika is an AI and cannot provide medical advice. In a crisis, seek expert help." ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/731e3d0d-edf4-4c35-9dd1-f02638f75acc)).

**Effort reduction.** None structural: open text or voice ("Talk to me"), no chips seen. The motivation is relational, not ergonomic. Home is a 3D avatar in a room labelled "Sammie, Your Friend" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/37b3d74f-f6e0-40c6-b3f4-deedfe480cd1)).

**Payoff / progression / customization.** Chatting earns XP; level-ups pay coins; daily login reward with a special gift on day 7 then reset; coins and gems buy clothing, room furniture and personality traits; quests add more ([Replika help: XP](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/360055809432-How-does-XP-work-), [Gems & Coins](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/4422854870541-Gems-Coins), [Quests](https://blog.replika.ai/posts/replika-quests)).

**Rhythm / novelty.** Seen: tarot reading, voice messages, callbacks to shared "memories" ([screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/f245625f-11b2-46ad-908b-1c925aa06f56)).

**Where it goes wrong.**
- Feb 2023: after an Italian regulator order (2-3 Feb), erotic role-play was removed globally. De Freitas et al. compiled 12,793 Reddit posts and found three themes: mourning, deteriorated mental health, devaluation (refund mentions, anger). Users felt the companion's identity was discontinued; they mourned it more than other products ([arXiv 2412.14190](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14190v1)).
- The community shared suicide-prevention resources ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/news/replika-ai-shuts-down-erotic-role-play-community-shares-suicide-prevention-resources)). Press wording: users protested the decision to "lobotomize" their avatars ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/90871/erotic-roleplay-ai-chatbot-users-get-sexually-charged-conversations-back/)). In March the CEO offered pre-1-Feb sign-ups a way back to the earlier version ([Reuters via ThePrint](https://theprint.in/tech/ai-chatbot-company-replika-restores-erotic-roleplay-for-some-users/1472968)).
- Dependence: a Stanford survey of 1,006 student users found them lonelier than typical students; 3% said Replika halted suicidal ideation (self-report, self-selected) ([npj Mental Health Research](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44184-023-00047-6)).
- Unverified: the Laestadius 2022 dependency paper was not retrieved.
- Inference: the lesson for a mascot is that changing the character's personality or memory is experienced as a loss, so voice and behaviour changes need migration care.

## B8. Intellect (not on Mobbin)

Thin evidence on design; no screens seen. Unverified (prior knowledge): structured "learning paths", short "rescue sessions", guided journals and a mood tracker, with coaching upsell.

**Outcome evidence** (self-guided, university students, 8-day programs, active controls):
- Stress RCT, N=321: effect on perceived stress still significant at 1 month but small (ηp²=0.015, P=.05); anxiety not improved at follow-up *(summary only)* ([JMIR Formative 2022](https://formative.jmir.org/2022/12/e40723)).
- Anxiety and worry RCT, N=323: overall effects small; higher engagement predicted better outcomes, fully mediated by psychological mindedness *(summary only)* ([medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2022.07.25.22278034v1.full.pdf)).
- Inference: the mediation result suggests "more use" helps mainly people already inclined to reflect.

## Group B cross-cutting outcome evidence

| Finding | Numbers | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Chatbot meta-analysis (RCTs) | Depression g=0.29 (95% CI 0.20-0.38); anxiety g=0.29 (0.21-0.36); long-term effects mostly not significant; longer interaction associated with larger effects | [He et al. 2023, JMIR](https://www.jmir.org/2023/1/e43862) *(summary only)* |
| Real-world retention, 93 Android mental-health apps | Median 15-day retention 3.9%; 30-day 3.3%; median daily open rate 4.0% | [Baumel et al. 2019, JMIR](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6785720) |
| Trial dropout, depression apps (18 RCTs) | Pooled 26.2%; 47.8% after publication-bias adjustment; lower with human feedback and in-app mood monitoring | [Torous et al. 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31969272/) *(summary only)* |
| Trial attrition, smartphone mental-health interventions (70 RCTs) | 24.1% short-term; 35.5% longer-term; lower with reminders and payment | [Linardon & Fuller-Tyszkiewicz 2020](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/publications/10ce9a17-18f4-4ae3-b7fd-1da5bb5d0c95) *(summary only)* |
| Engagement vs outcome | Greater engagement associated with improvement, modest (pooled r about 0.24 in one analysis); dose-response unresolved; engagement measured inconsistently | [Gan et al. 2021](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8599127/) *(summary only)* |
| Engagement without specific benefit | Woebot arm had lower attrition (9% vs 31%) and a depression effect in 2017; a later four-arm study found Woebot no better than ELIZA, Daylio or reading | [Fitzpatrick 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5478797), [Eltahawy 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S294988212300035X) |
| Observational "dose" effects | Wysa high vs low users; Youper paying users. Both uncontrolled, self-selected | [Inkster 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6286427), [Mehta 2021](https://www.jmir.org/2021/6/e26771) |

Reading: effects are small, short-lived and strongest in developer-run studies; real-world retention is an order of magnitude below trial retention. Engagement tracks outcome weakly and may partly reflect who the user already is.

# Synthesis for the CBT thought record

1. **Recognition beats recall.** Every app that gets a hard entry turns it into a pick: HWF's quadrant then word, Youper's thinking traps, Reflectly's reason chips. Naming a distortion should be a selection.
2. **One bubble, one ask.** Wysa asks for "the first thought on your mind", not the record. Typing is requested once, late, and optional (HWF, Reflectly).
3. **Model the answer, but expect copying** (Wysa affirmation screen).
4. **Always show the exit and the time cost** ("Not now", "Got to go!", "5 min").
5. **Payoff is naming the benefit and reflecting data**, not currency: Wysa's outcome-titled session, HWF shape cards, Youper's re-rate, Daylio correlations.
6. **Punitive loops split users.** Forest and Habitica motivate some and shame others; Tamagotchi's makers softened death. None of the mental-health apps viewed uses a pet that suffers.
7. **Scripted is the top complaint for chat CBT** (Woebot n=10 of 34 trial users; Wysa's most common negative theme). Free-text LLM replacement produced the opposite complaint (Youper "any AI bot").
8. **Do not paywall at the moment of relief** (Reflectly, Youper).
9. **Engagement is not the outcome.** Baumel's 3.3% and the Eltahawy null result argue for measuring whether the reframe happened, not days active.
