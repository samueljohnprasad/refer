### Priority Issues

- **[P2] The "Hero Metric" Trope**
  - **Why it matters**: A massive, colored serif number feels loud and performative, clashing with the introspective nature of the exercise.
  - **Fix**: Tone down the number. Shift it to `color="ink"` instead of `sage`, and potentially switch to a sans-serif variant or reduce the scale to feel more grounded.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable typeset`

- **[P3] Conflicting CSS Classes on Labels**
  - **Why it matters**: The min/max labels have `variant="caption-muted"` (which applies `text-ink-muted`) but also declare `color="soft"` and `className="font-semibold"`. This overrides the intended caption styling and creates muddy contrast.
  - **Fix**: Remove `color="soft"` and `className="font-semibold"` from the labels, letting the design system's `caption-muted` variant drive the style.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`
