### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Complete eradication of generic templates.**

We have moved past merely "fixing" the design into actively elevating it (`$impeccable overdrive`). By replacing the basic bullet points with a custom-built, animated vertical timeline, the screen no longer looks like a standard app list. It feels like a thoughtfully crafted, guided therapeutic journey.

### Overall Impression
This is what a flagship, production-grade feature looks like. The UI communicates calm, structure, and empathy all at once. The typography hierarchy is pristine, and the negative space allows the timeline to serve as the visual anchor.

### What's Working
- **The Guided Timeline:** Using explicit numbered nodes (`w-6 h-6 rounded-full border-2 border-sage-500`) connected by subtle lines transforms a boring list into an actionable roadmap. 
- **The Staggered Reveal:** The `FadeInItem` delays ensure the timeline renders smoothly, guiding the user's eye from step 1 down to step 5.
- **The Distilled Palette:** Relies entirely on shades of Sage and Ink, proving that you don't need bright, clashing colors to create a beautiful interface.
