### Anti-Patterns Verdict
- **AI Slop Background**: The full-screen warm beige gradient (`MOOD_GRADIENTS`) is the #1 saturated AI default of 2026. This strictly violates `DESIGN.md`, which mandates a restrained, calming palette of sage, white, and dark green ink without generic gradient backgrounds.
- **"AI Summary" Label**: Naming a reflection literally "AI Summary" in a formal Cormorant serif font screams corporate automation and surveillance. It breaks the calm therapy notebook trust.
- **Ghost Cards**: Both the mood icon badge and the summary container (`bg-white/40` over yellow) exhibit ghost card tells (pairing borders with soft drop shadows or mushy low-contrast fills).
- **Over-rounding**: The summary card relies on `rounded-3xl` (>24px, appearing around ~32px), violating our hard component geometry ceiling (≤24px).

### Overall Impression
The typography pairing (Cormorant headings with Geist body text) provides a legitimate editorial foundation, but the interface sabotages its own therapeutic credibility through AI slop defaults—specifically the warm beige gradient, ghost card geometries, and clinical "AI Summary" surveillance copy.

### What's Working
1. **Typography Discipline**: Using Cormorant for reflective titles paired with Geist for operational/body prose creates intentional editorial contrast without font soup.
2. **Spatial Rhythm**: Comfortable reading padding and line length within the body prose keeps the entry scannable and uncrowded.
3. **Unobtrusive Toolbar**: Clustering top actions into a clean, floating pill keeps the reading surface focused and uncluttered.

### Priority Issues
- **[P0] Tonal Disconnect ("AI Summary" naming)**: Titled "AI Summary", this section breaks private therapeutic trust and feels like clinical robot surveillance.
  - *Why it matters*: Users journaling vulnerable thoughts feel clinically evaluated rather than quietly supported in a safe space.
  - *Fix*: Rename heading to "Themes" or "Reflection", or drop the heading completely to present it as a quiet editorial marginalia note.
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable clarify`
- **[P1] Brand Palette Violation (Cream Beige Gradient)**: Background uses a generic warm beige gradient instead of official brand tokens (`canvas`, `surface`, sage tints).
  - *Why it matters*: Erodes the distinctive identity of a high-end mental health journal, making it look like a templated AI wrapper.
  - *Fix*: Bind backgrounds strictly to the core sage/white tokens defined in `lib/tokens.ts` and `DESIGN.md`.
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable colorize`
- **[P2] Surface Geometry & Radius Over-rounding**: Summary card exceeds our 24px corner limit and mood badge pairs border plus drop shadow.
  - *Why it matters*: Arbitrary bubbly geometries project a floaty, uncrafted feel.
  - *Fix*: Enforce a hard ceiling of 16–24px radius max and convert ghost cards to clean tonal fills or solid 1px borders.
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable polish`
- **[P3] Missing User Control on Summary**: No affordance to dismiss, edit, or regenerate a synthesized reflection.
  - *Why it matters*: If the synthesis misinterprets the user's emotions, they are permanently stuck with an inaccurate reflection of their internal state.
  - *Fix*: Implement a swipe-to-dismiss gesture or subtle menu item to remove unwanted reflection notes.
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable shape`
