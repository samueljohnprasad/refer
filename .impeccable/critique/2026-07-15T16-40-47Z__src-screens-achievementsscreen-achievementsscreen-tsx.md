#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: This screen suffers from two major AI-design tells:
1. **Identical Card Grids**: The "Your Progress" section is a stack of three identical `StatCard`s wrapped in white borders. This is a very common, generic AI output when asked to display stats.
2. **The Hero-Metric Template**: Each card uses the same layout: an icon, a title, a subtitle, and a big value on the right. 

Additionally, the typography is clashing heavily. The page title ("Achievements") is a massive sans-serif (`Outfit-Bold`), but the section headers ("Your Progress", "All Badges") and the values ("10", "1000", "0/5") are using a serif font (`happy-font-heading-bold`). This reads as mismatched rather than intentional.

#### Overall Impression
The screen feels like a wireframe generated by an LLM that hasn't been designed. The repeated cards create unnecessary visual borders and noise. 

#### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Repeated Ghost Cards**: The three stats in "Your Progress" are wrapped in borders and backgrounds.
  - **Why it matters**: It's the "lazy answer" for layout. It creates bounding boxes that trap the eye and scream "templated UI".
  - **Fix**: Flatten the structure. Remove the outer borders and background from the `StatCard`. Use a simple, airy list, or place them in a single subtle surface without dividers. Let the whitespace and typography do the work.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill` or `$impeccable quieter`

- **[P1] Typographic Mismatch**: Mixing heavy sans-serif page headers with serif section headers and numbers.
  - **Why it matters**: It looks broken. A brand can mix serif and sans-serif, but it must be applied systematically. 
  - **Fix**: Re-evaluate the font families used on the `<Text>` tags. Ensure the section headers match the visual weight of the content.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable typeset`
