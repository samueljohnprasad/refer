#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Low Contrast on Subtext**: 
  - **Why it matters**: The `text-ink-muted` class on the subtext ("Write down your thoughts...") is washing out against the white background. Users with low vision, or users outside in the sun, won't be able to read it. Gray text for "elegance" is a common trap that ruins legibility.
  - **Fix**: Bump the text color closer to the ink end of the scale (e.g., use `text-ink` with a slight opacity, or a darker gray token).
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish` (or `$impeccable colorize`)
